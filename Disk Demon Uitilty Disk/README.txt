Disk Demon Utility Disk
25 September 2026

*** NOT copy protected ***

The Disk Demon Utility disk was an optional extra for the Disk Demon and sold by Datel in the UK

The Disk Demon Utility disk contains 4 programs
filecopier (a modified version of the original filemanager program)
diskcopier
multicopier
instructions

The programs all seem to work ok although only in a Disk Demon or Professional DOS system.

The disk image is called DiskDemonUtility.d64
